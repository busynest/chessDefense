# Playtest report

108 AI-vs-AI games after Easy was given a pawn spend. 12 seeds, every pairing of easy, normal, and hard. Nothing unfinished, nothing thrown, no desync.

## What changed

- Easy no longer sits on a full bank. When Black has at least 64 gold, the next decision upgrades a pawn. Pawns only: rooks, bishops, the Queen, and the King stay at level 1. At 96 gold, Easy also buys a wall, up to four. The random skip still applies to everything else, so Easy does not play at Normal's pace.
- Overcharge is unchanged from the previous batch: 36 gold the first time on a tower, then 28 more each time, 8 seconds of boost, 30 seconds of cooldown. Easy still does not buy it.

Gold spent is **96%** of gold gained. Overcharges: **1,078**, **10.0 per game**.

Time at the 120 gold cap is **35%** of side-ticks, down from **40%** before the Easy spend. Easy vs Easy fell from **81%** to **72%**. Easy against Normal or Hard fell from about **40%** to **18–28%**, because those games end before Easy can refill the bank. The leftover cap on Easy mirrors is the gold that arrives after every pawn is already level 3.

## For Jack

- Easy is still Easy. It spends on pawns and a few walls, and it loses every game to Normal and Hard.
- A further sink for a level-3 pawn town would have to be something other than a stronger piece, or Easy stops being the short match.
